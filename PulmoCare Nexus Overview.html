What it is

  • An administrative and research-operations concept.
  • A working front-end prototype you can click through.
  • Built to show how technology can fit a physician's practice.

What it is not

  • Not a clinical, diagnostic or treatment tool.
  • Not an EHR, and no real integrations exist.
  • Not validated for HIPAA or any regulatory approval.
  • All names and numbers are fictional.
